Honest progress.

You don't need to turn every page into a card. Start with the ideas that matter, and make a little room to practise recalling them.

A few things worth knowing
  1. 01

    Keep one clear idea per card.

    A question, a concept, a useful example. Short and specific is easier to practise.

  2. 02

    Recall before you reveal.

    Give yourself a moment to find the answer, rather than just recognising it on the page.

  3. 03

    Tell it how the review really went.

    Forgot, partly remembered, remembered or easy. Honest feedback makes the next review more useful.

  4. 04

    Come back to Today.

    Review what's ready, catch up when you miss a day, and let your activity show the habit growing.

LET'S CLEAR A FEW THINGS UP

Curious?
Good place to start.

A little clarity before your first card.

What exactly is Retainzy?

Retainzy is a learning and knowledge-retention workspace. You collect useful ideas as cards, organise them, and revisit them through scheduled reviews. AI tools help you work with your documents and saved knowledge; your own recall and feedback keep the learning active.

How does the AI support my learning?

Gemini powers AI-assisted PDF-to-card drafts, conversational draft refinement, the knowledge assistant, and recall-test feedback. You can check source context, ask about your saved cards and progress, or get feedback on points your recall answer missed. The knowledge assistant is focused on your Retainzy workspace—not a general-purpose chatbot.

Does AI automatically change my cards or review schedule?

The knowledge assistant is read-only. You review and approve document-generated drafts before saving them as cards. AI recall tests provide scores, improvement suggestions and possible misconceptions; they do not automatically replace your review rating or change your schedule. Always check AI-generated content against the source.

Do I have to create every card myself?

No. You can write your own cards, generate drafts from a PDF with Gemini, or save public cards you discover in the community. AI drafts are editable, so you can check the source, correct anything that needs work, and keep only useful cards.

What happens when I forget a card?

Mark it “Forgot” honestly. Retainzy uses review feedback to bring difficult cards back sooner and give well-remembered cards more space. Missed reviews remain available in your overdue queue. No tool can guarantee you will never forget, but regular, honest practice gives you a useful review routine.

Are my cards automatically shared?

No. New cards are private by default. You choose whether to make a card public for other learners to discover. You can organise and review your own collection without publishing it.

How should I use it effectively?

Start with a few short cards, each focused on one idea. Try recalling before reading the answer. Give honest feedback rather than marking everything Easy, and return to Today regularly. Check AI-generated drafts before saving, and use your focus areas to guide extra practice.

How do I get started?

Choose Log in or Start learning, then sign in with Google or register with email. Create your first card or upload a PDF, organise it into a topic, and open Today for your first review. The preview on this page is just a demo; it does not save cards or change your account.
